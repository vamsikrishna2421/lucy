What is LUCY?

LUCY is a private AI memory assistant that lives entirely on your device. It captures your thoughts, organizes your memories, and proactively surfaces what matters — like a thoughtful friend who never forgets anything you told them. No cloud sync. No account required. Everything stays on your phone.

The Problem We're Solving

You have hundreds of useful thoughts, decisions, tasks, and ideas every day. Most of them disappear. You forget what you said in a meeting. You forget the name of that tool someone recommended. You forget you said you'd come back to something important.

Existing tools require too much effort — apps that need you to format things, tag things, categorize things. LUCY's answer: just say it. Speak naturally, type casually. LUCY figures out what matters and brings it back when you need it.

Core Features

1. Capture — Say or type anything

The home screen (Board tab) shows your pending work and a text input at the bottom. Type anything naturally — a thought, a task, something that happened, a reminder.

Golden rule: The more context you give, the better LUCY understands. "Called Sam" is weak. "Called Sam about the Q3 budget review, he'll send the spreadsheet by Friday" is what LUCY can actually work with.

What LUCY automatically extracts

Tasks

Things you need to do, with urgency levels. Appear on your Board.

Reminders

Scheduled notifications — fires 15, 10, 5 minutes before, then at the time.

Expenses

"Paid 9 dollars for lunch" → logged automatically with category.

Decisions

"I decided to cancel my gym membership" → stored for recall.

People

Names mentioned are tracked and connected across captures.

Loose ends

"I'll come back to this later" → saved for follow-up in Today tab.

Follow-ups

"I asked Sam to review the doc" → tracked until resolved.

Ideas

Startup or creative ideas — always stored privately, never sent to cloud.

2. Board — Your personal whiteboard

The first tab (Board) shows all your pending tasks organized by context — office work, personal, urgent. It's designed to feel like a whiteboard you glance at in the morning.

4. Ask LUCY — Query your memory

The Ask tab lets you have a conversation with your memories. LUCY reads your recent captures and answers from them using AI.

Example questions that work well

  • "What did I work on today?"
  • "Summarize my meetings this week"
  • "What tasks do I have for the office project?"
  • "How much did I spend this month?"
  • "What's connected to Sam?"
  • "Yes, add to my progress that I finished the report" → LUCY saves it to memory

For best results: Enable Remote Intelligence in Settings → add your OpenAI API key. This gives LUCY access to GPT for smarter, more conversational answers. Without it, the on-device model is used (less accurate for complex questions).

5. Passive Listening — Always-on capture

Tap the Listen button in the header to start passive listening mode. LUCY will continuously record and transcribe what's being said around you — designed for office hours, meetings, and continuous work sessions.

FeatureDetail
BatchingTranscripts are bundled every 10 minutes and processed through LUCY's AI pipeline
BackgroundKeeps recording even when you switch to other apps (red indicator shows on iOS)
Music detectionIdentifies songs you're humming via ShazamKit — appears in Today → Now with Spotify link
PrivacyOn-device transcription when available; Whisper API as fallback (requires OpenAI key)
LanguagesAuto-detects — works for Telugu, Tanglish, English, Hindi, and mixed language

Note: iOS requires a visible red recording indicator while passive listening is active. This is an Apple requirement and cannot be hidden. LUCY will not record silently without your knowledge.

7. Memory Correction

If LUCY extracted something incorrectly from a capture:

  1. Go to Today → Captured
  2. Find the memory and tap the ? button (top-right of the card)
  3. Type what's wrong or what LUCY should know instead
  4. Tap Send to LUCY — she'll re-process with your correction

Privacy & Data

No account required

LUCY works fully offline. No login, no signup.

Encrypted storage

All data is stored in SQLite with encryption (SQLCipher) on your device.

Ideas always private

Startup and creative ideas never leave your device, even with remote AI on.

Remote AI masking

If remote AI is enabled, private captures are first masked by on-device AI before any data leaves your phone.

What to Test

As a beta tester, please focus on these areas and report what you find:

Board (home screen)

  • Capture a task with context ("Call the landlord tomorrow morning about the lease") — does it appear correctly grouped on the Board?
  • Mark a task as done — does the animation play smoothly? Does it move to "Done today"?
  • Tap ⋯ on a task — can you edit the text or delete it?
  • Tap Undo — does the task come back correctly?

Reminders

  • Capture "Remind me at [time ~15 min from now] to check my email" — do you get cascade notifications (15, 10, 5 min before + at time)?
  • Does the notification detail screen (tap the notification) show a natural explanation?

Ask LUCY

  • Ask "What did I work on today?" — does it give a real answer from your notes?
  • Ask "Yes, add that I finished the design review today" — does LUCY confirm it saved?
  • Check that responses have no **asterisks** (bold markdown) showing in the text

Memory correction

  • Go to Today → Captured → find a memory → tap the ? button — does the correction modal open?
  • Type a correction and send — does LUCY acknowledge it?

Passive listening

  • Tap Listen — does it start (red indicator, word count increases)?
  • Speak for 2-3 minutes — does the transcript appear in Captured after stopping?
  • Does the word count in the header increment while speaking?

General quality

  • Are there duplicate tasks on the Board?
  • Do notifications fire at the right times?
  • Does the splash screen appear on first launch (cold start)?
  • Is the app responsive and smooth throughout?
